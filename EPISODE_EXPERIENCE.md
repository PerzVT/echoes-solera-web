# Episode and interactive experience

Death & Desire is designed for mobile viewing and portrait video. The app viewer keeps the picture uncropped, with episode navigation in a sheet. Desktop adds an adjacent episode rail.

- Explicit new, in-progress, completed, next, paid, unlocked and caught-up states.
- Episode-specific stills taken from the supplied full-length media. Original Part numbering, including 3.5, is preserved.
- Search covers Originals and Explore. Back restores the originating list/query.
- Local demo purchases are committed before success is shown. Creator revenue shares and platform business terms remain provisional.
- Creator drafts use the consumer series/player templates. Scheduling requires video and coherent dates. Draft removal and episode ordering are available.
- Solera guards loading and fast double taps, restores branch context through history, provides resume/restart/series return, and exposes seek/time controls.
- App/Web preview keeps its iframe mounted through temporary zero-size measurements.

The pitch deck is being developed in a separate task from forthcoming Figma references. Do not assume the earlier full-payout concept or illustrative funding fee establishes final creator or Originals terms.
